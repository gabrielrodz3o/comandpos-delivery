# Delivery — moto de arranque

`delivery-scooter-v2.png` es una ilustración 3D prerenderizada, con transparencia, creada para ComandPOS Delivery mediante la herramienta integrada `image_gen`. La app anima su entrada con Reanimated; no utiliza un modelo 3D interactivo ni descarga imágenes al arrancar.

## Prompt de generación

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: premium 3D scooter illustration for the startup animation of ComandPOS Delivery, a professional mobile delivery app. This is the standalone scooter asset, NOT a screen mockup.
Primary request: create a beautifully crafted, believable miniature delivery scooter with a compact insulated delivery box attached behind the saddle. Real three dimensional industrial design, soft sculpted forms, not a flat extruded icon, not clipart. Modern understated European electric scooter design, no rider.
Composition: single scooter, entire vehicle clearly visible and centered, facing to the RIGHT in a restrained three-quarter front view, camera at slightly above wheel height, front wheel toward lower right, rear cargo box toward upper left. Wheels grounded on the same plane. Object occupies about 82% of a square canvas, with generous clean padding so mirrors and tyres are not cut off. Keep the silhouette strong at small mobile sizes.
Materials: warm porcelain ivory painted body, rich ComandPOS orange (#F97316) lower fairing and delivery box, charcoal rubber tyres with realistic subtle tread, satin aluminium wheel hubs, graphite saddle, precise small chrome details, clear circular headlamp. Delivery box has an ivory inset panel with a simple embossed orange checkmark, no text or lettering.
Lighting: high end product studio render, large soft key from upper left, warm gentle fill from right, subtle edge highlights and ambient occlusion, restrained polished reflections, crisp refined bevels. Refined designer product miniature, not childish inflated toy. Render similar quality to a professional Blender/Cinema4D product campaign.
Background: truly transparent alpha background, no background color, no ground plane, no baked ground shadow (a separate animated shadow will be added in the app), no environment, no checkerboard pattern. Transparent cutout of only the scooter.
Constraints: no text, no logos except the simple checkmark, no watermarks, no additional props, no people, no scenery, no speed lines, no platform, no glow. Square high resolution PNG with actual transparent alpha.

## Ajuste final

Keep the exact same scooter, materials, lighting, and transparent alpha. Zoom out to include both mirror tips and both complete tyres, with 12% transparent margins on all sides. No baked shadow, scenery, lettering, or background.

## Referencias de movimiento
